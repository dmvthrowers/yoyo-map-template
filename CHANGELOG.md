## 1.0.0

The first tagged release: a privacy-first community map built from one settings file.

- A map with square pins in your own categories, filter checkboxes, and a list of everyone that works without JavaScript.
- People are published at city level only; the build refuses addresses, emails, phone numbers and ages.
- Works for any hobby: category labels, colors and wording all come from the settings file; three example maps.
- Canonical links, social tags, structured data, a sitemap and robots.txt once the public address is known.
- Leaflet is vendored, so the page loads nothing but its own files and the map tiles.
